# Code of conduct

Inkdrift's community should be a place for precise, respectful technical work.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and considerate, especially when someone lacks context.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Respect different platforms, experience levels, languages, and accessibility needs.
- Share only content you have the right and permission to share.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, sexualized attention, and disclosure of private credentials or personal data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe. Report concerns to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not post another person's private information in a public report.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
